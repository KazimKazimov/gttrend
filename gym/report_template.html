US Treasury 10-year · monthly · real-time evaluation

Which variables help call 10Y trends in real time?

A walk-forward gym scores trend detectors that only see data published by each month-end, against the ex-post up / sideways / down labels of method D. Every candidate variable is tested twice on the same months and the same training rows, with and without it.

Model leaderboard

All detectors on one window. "Own" models see only the 10Y's history; "all X" models see every variable available in the window. Models with trained parameters share the same first training month, set by the latest-starting variable.

Window

Median delay: months from a trend's first month to the first correct call (negative means called before it began, possible only for forecasts). Capture: share of the trend's full move still ahead when it was called. P&L: long duration when the call is down, short when up, flat when sideways, month-end to month-end, bp of yield per year (nowcasts only).

Delay versus false alarms

A probabilistic detector can wait for confidence before it switches: the call moves to a new state only when that state's probability reaches a threshold. Each curve sweeps the threshold from plain argmax (the most jumpy point) to 0.90; the rule machines are single points. Down and to the left is better. Hollow markers miss more than 10% of the trends, so a short delay there is not a win.

Window

Each cell: median delay in months · false alarms per 10 years · share of trends called. The thresholds were not chosen out of sample; read the table as a menu of trade-offs, and compare windows L and M for stability.

Does the variable turn before the 10Y?

Ex-post trends of each variable, dated with the same retracement machine on a volatility-scaled threshold, matched to the 10Y's trend starts within ±12 months (1978 onward). A positive lead means the variable turned first. The small charts show concordance, the share of months in which the 10Y's state equals the variable's state k months earlier. Unemployment, the credit spread and the VIX are sign-flipped, because they usually fall when yields trend up.

How the gym works

Information set

At each month-end the detector sees the real-time panel: market series through that month, macro series lagged by their release delay (CPI, payrolls, unemployment, PCE, Cleveland Fed: one month). A missed release keeps the last published value.

Training labels

Every 12 months the detector is refit on labels computed from the 10Y up to that date only (vintage labels), never on the final labels, which use future data.

Forecasts

The frozen model gives P(down, sideways, up) for the current month (nowcast) and for 3 and 6 months ahead (ex ante), every month of the next year.

Scoring

Forecasts are scored against the final ex-post labels, only on months whose label is settled (before the last, still-open segment).

Matched comparison

A variable's effect is the difference between the model with and without it, trained on the same rows and scored on the same months, with Diebold–Mariano, block-bootstrap and Wilcoxon tests.

No look-ahead, tested

Unit tests rerun each detector on data cut at 2008 and require identical forecasts up to the cut; features computed on cut data must equal those computed on full data.

Literature survey

Three families of methods use outside variables to detect or anticipate regime changes. The gym implements the first two in full and a simple member of the third.

Data

Monthly panel, 1947 to August 2026, built by scripts/fetch_public_data.py from public GitHub mirrors of FRED, CBOE, EIA, BLS, Shiller and H.10 files (FRED itself was not reachable from the build environment). Drop your own series into the same layout to test them.
